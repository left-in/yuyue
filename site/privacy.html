Yuyue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 20 日

In short

Yuyue (语阅) is a free reading and listening app with no servers of its own. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Where your data lives

Your books, reading progress, highlights, notes, word lists and settings are stored on your own device. If you turn on sync, they travel only between your devices and the storage you choose yourself — iCloud Drive, a WebDAV server or Google Drive. The developer never receives or sees them.

When the app goes online

Only for things you set up yourself:

  • Sync with the storage you chose.
  • Remote libraries (OPDS or WebDAV) you added.
  • The reading companion with your own API key: your question and a few related passages from the book go directly to the AI provider you picked. Without a key, the companion uses the model on your device.
  • Cloud voices with your own key: the sentences to be read go directly to the voice provider you picked (OpenAI, Microsoft Azure or ElevenLabs). Audio already received is kept on your device.
  • Note export to Notion or flomo with your own token: the highlights you send go directly to that service.

Those requests go from your device straight to the provider; their own privacy terms apply. Your keys and passwords are stored only on your device, in the system keychain (Apple) or encrypted storage (Android), and are never synced or uploaded.

On-device features

Speech recognition (to line up an audiobook with its e-book), translation, read-aloud with system voices and the dictionary all run on your device.

Children

Yuyue collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: yuyue.app@icloud.com

一句话

语阅是一款免费的读书、听书软件，没有自己的服务器，不收集、不保存、不出售、不分享任何关于你的数据。没有账号、没有统计、没有追踪、没有广告。

数据存在哪

书、进度、划线、想法、生词和设置都存在你自己的设备上。打开同步时，它们只在你的设备和你自己选的网盘（iCloud 云盘、WebDAV 或 Google Drive）之间传，开发者收不到，也看不到。

什么时候联网

只在你自己设置了下面这些的时候：

  • 同步：连你选的网盘。
  • 远程书库：连你添加的 OPDS 或 WebDAV 书库。
  • 书伴：填了自己的 Key 时，你的提问和书里相关的几段原文直接发给你选的服务商；没填 Key 时用设备自带的模型，不联网。
  • 云端声音：填了自己的 Key 时，要读的句子直接发给你选的服务商（OpenAI、微软 Azure 或 ElevenLabs）；读过的声音存在你的设备上。
  • 笔记导出：填了 Notion 或 flomo 的令牌时，你发出去的划线直接发给那个服务。

这些请求都从你的设备直接发给服务商，适用它们自己的隐私条款。Key 和密码只存在你的设备上（苹果设备的钥匙串、Android 的加密存储），不同步、不上传。

在设备上完成的

有声书和电子书对齐时的语音转文字、翻译、系统声音朗读、查词，都在你的设备上完成。

儿童

语阅不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。
